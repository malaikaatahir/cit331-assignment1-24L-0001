Who we are

BeBaak means the light of women. We are a student-led club that turns confidence into action through events, mentoring and creative projects.

What we do

  • Host talks and panels with women leaders from many careers.
  • Run skill workshops in technology, public speaking and finance.
  • Celebrate South Asian art, craft and culture.
  • Build a supportive network that lasts beyond graduation.

Contact the committee

Questions, ideas or want to volunteer? Email bebaak@university.edu.pk or visit the Student Affairs office.
